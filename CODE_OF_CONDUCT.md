# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our code of conduct.

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at [saf@mitre.org](mailto:saf@mitre.org). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

For the full text of our code of conduct, please see: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
